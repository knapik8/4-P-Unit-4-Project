Wars of Israel

Context

The focus of this website will be the 6 Day War and the Yom Kippur War. Obviously my summaries will be brief and I won't be able to fully delve into these conflicts due to their deep complexity. I hope you at least learn something, and if you'd like to discuss anything further with me, I'd be thrilled to.

Preceding these wars was the Arab-Israeli War of 1948. On the midnight of May 14th, Britain's Mandate for Palestine expired, the European nation finally pulling out of the land after some 28 years. The Mandate, along with others for different territories, was created after WWI by the League of Nations when distributing said territories from one nation to another.

On this same night, David Ben-Gurion declared Israel's independence and formal existence as a new country. He would become Israel's first Prime Minister.

An important document to note is the Balfour Declaration of 1917; a British declaration stating that Israel would eventually be established as a national homeland for Jews. This declaration inspired migration of European Jews to Israel/Palestine, as well as other push factors like religious persecution and a rise in Zionist ideology. Both the increasing Jewish and British presence in Palestine upset the Palestinian Arabs who had been living there for generations and generations.

Another document to note was the United Nations Partiton Plan for Palestine created in 1947. It basically split the land bicamerally; one half for Arabs, the other for Jews. While Jewish leaders accepted this plan, the Arab leaders did not.
